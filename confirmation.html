Thanks for Exploring PesoWise!

PesoWise is a student budgeting website prototype that uses JavaScript to process your entries, calculate budget totals, track expenses, and display savings goal progress.

Your information is saved in your browser using local storage, allowing you to revisit your entries in the same browser. Your data is not connected to an online database or a real financial account.

STAY SAFE

Protect Your Financial Information

Build good habits whenever you use budgeting tools.

  • Keep personal details private. Avoid entering passwords, bank account numbers, or other sensitive information into the website.
  • Be mindful of your spending. Review your expenses and identify areas where you may be able to save.
  • Set realistic goals. Choose savings targets that fit your allowance and everyday needs.
  • Understand browser storage. Your entries are saved locally in your browser. Clearing browser data or using a different browser may remove or make your saved entries unavailable.
  • Remember the limitations. PesoWise is an educational budgeting prototype. It does not connect to banks, process payments, or provide professional financial advice.
