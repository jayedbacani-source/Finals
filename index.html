Budget With Awareness

PesoWise is a student budgeting website prototype for educational purposes. It uses JavaScript to calculate budget totals, track recorded expenses, and display savings goal progress. Your entries are saved in your browser using local storage and are not connected to an online database or real financial accounts. Avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information.
